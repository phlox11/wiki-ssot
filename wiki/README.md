# Wiki

`status: current` pages under this directory are the single source of truth for this repository's intent, architecture, contracts, invariants, and operations.

- Start at [index.md](./index.md) (generated).
- Drill into the complete [catalog.md](./catalog.md) (generated) when the bounded index points to a group or lifecycle.
- Ask what remains through [work-queue.md](./work-queue.md) (generated) or `bun run wiki:work`.
- The page contract is [SCHEMA.md](./SCHEMA.md).
- The change process is [WORKFLOW.md](./WORKFLOW.md).
- Open conflicts are in [conflicts.md](./conflicts.md) (generated).

## Accumulation and history

- `status: current` pages are mutable snapshots of the current product and engineering contract.
- `status: proposed` pages carry future intent; their structured work items retain acceptance and durable done evidence.
- Open conflict pages are resolution contracts, and resolved conflict pages retain the decision and its evidence.
- `changelog.md` records selected current-contract milestones rather than every repository change.
- Git is the history of record for ordinary changes.

`current-status.md` exposes cumulative counts without making done work or resolved conflicts current authority. Use `bun run wiki:work -- --all` and `bun run wiki:conflicts -- --all` for their complete lifecycle views.

`index.md`, `catalog.md`, `current-status.md`, `work-queue.md`, `conflicts.md`, `.wiki/relationship-graph.json`, and the reverse maps are generated by `bun run wiki:generated` — do not edit them by hand.
